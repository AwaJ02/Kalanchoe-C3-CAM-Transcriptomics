# KEGG Mapper Visualisations

This directory contains KEGG Mapper visualisations of the carbon fixation
pathway across the six diel sampling timepoints in *Kalanchoë fedtschenkoi*.

Maps are provided for:

- 10 h
- 14 h
- 18 h
- 22 h
- 02 h
- 06 h

Genes showing significant differential expression between LP6 and LP1 were
mapped to KEGG Orthology (KO) identifiers and visualised on the KEGG carbon
fixation pathway.

## Colour convention

- Blue = genes with higher expression in LP6 (mature CAM leaves)
- Red = genes with higher expression in LP1 (young, predominantly C3 leaves)
- Uncoloured = no significant differentially expressed mapped KO

These pathway maps provide a visual representation of transcriptional
differences and should not be interpreted as direct measurements of
metabolic flux or enzyme activity.
